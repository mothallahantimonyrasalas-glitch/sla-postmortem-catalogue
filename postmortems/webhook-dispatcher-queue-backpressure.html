INC-403: Webhook Dispatcher Queue Backpressure

Service: Event Broker | Impact Tier: SEV-1 | Date: Mon Oct 05 2026 00:00:00 GMT+0530 (India Standard Time)

Root Cause Summary

Downstream partner endpoint latency exhausted worker thread pool and stalled outbound webhooks.

Mitigation Steps

  1. Isolate slow tenant queue
  2. Enable circuit breaker at 2000ms
  3. Scale consumer pod replicas to 16
  4. Replay dead-letter queue
