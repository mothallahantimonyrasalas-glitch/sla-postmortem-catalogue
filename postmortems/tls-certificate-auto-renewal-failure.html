INC-405: TLS Certificate Auto-Renewal Failure

Service: Edge Ingress | Impact Tier: SEV-3 | Date: Sat Oct 10 2026 00:00:00 GMT+0530 (India Standard Time)

Root Cause Summary

ACME DNS-01 challenge timed out due to stale TXT record lock in secondary DNS provider.

Mitigation Steps

  1. Clear stale TXT record
  2. Force cert-manager renewal
  3. Sync TLS secret across ingress pods
  4. Add 14-day expiry pager alert
